Present conditions

Descent IAS is 290 KIAS by default; pick 280 or 270 (or type 250–320) when the STAR or ATC assigns a speed. Idle rate depends on this speed and your weight: slower or heavier means a shallower idle path, so you start down earlier. Wind is plus for a tailwind, minus for a headwind. Descent Mach is 0.74 until you reach the high-speed IAS. 250 KIAS at 10,000 ft is always built in, and a tighter restriction of yours overrides it.

Arrival

US STARs fill in from the FAA CIFP. Your Canadian airports are in the picker, but NAV CANADA does not publish a free coded STAR file, so those still have to be entered by hand from the CAP or your Jeppesen chart. After a fill you can change any restriction (type, altitude, speed) for this plan only; the arrival database is not changed. Edited fixes are marked, and Reset to published puts the chart value back.

Arrival waypoints

Waypoint 1 is the end of the STAR or last constraint. Add further fixes working backwards up the arrival. Each leg is the distance to the previously entered fix. The plan is shown in flying order.
